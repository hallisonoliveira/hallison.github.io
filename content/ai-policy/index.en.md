This blog is written under the author's responsibility. When a generative artificial intelligence tool takes part in an article, the **AI Usage** badge at the end of the text states, plainly, which stage it was used in.

The badge describes the tool's participation. It does not replace authorship, editorial responsibility, fact-checking, or the views expressed in the article.

## Badge categories

### None

No generative AI was used to create, research, review, translate, code, or make images for that post.

### Review

An AI tool was used to review grammar, clarity, organization, or style. Editorial decisions and the published version remain the author's.

### Translation

An AI tool assisted with translating the content. The translation is reviewed before publication.

### Code

An AI tool generated or assisted with code examples. The author evaluates the examples before publishing them.

### Visual

An AI tool generated or edited images used in the post. This badge does not apply to images created without generative AI.

### Research

An AI tool assisted with locating or organizing references. Sources and relevant claims remain subject to the author's evaluation.

### Assistance

An AI tool took part in drafting portions of the content. This category indicates a more direct contribution to the writing, always reviewed and owned by the author.

## Editorial commitment

The author is responsible for selecting sources, for the information's best possible accuracy, for the opinions presented, and for each article's final version. The badge exists to make the process clear without turning use of a tool into a claim of authorship.
